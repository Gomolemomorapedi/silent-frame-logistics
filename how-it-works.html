A clear route from request to delivery

How it works.

Silent Frame Logistics is preparing a marketplace for customers who need goods moved and transport providers with a suitable vehicle. The initial service area is Krugersdorp and the West Rand.

For customers

Request the right transport.

01 / DESCRIBE

Share the load and route.

Enter collection and delivery areas, load details, preferred dates, stops and number of days.

02 / REVIEW

Compare a provider's offer.

Suitable providers will review the job and make an offer. A private cost recommendation is not the customer price.

03 / CONFIRM

Agree terms before checkout.

Review the final price, provider, timing and cancellation terms before any future card payment.

04 / COMPLETE

Track the agreed job status.

The planned service will record collection, delivery evidence and completion before applying the payout rules.

For transporters

Make an informed offer.

01 / REGISTER

Share your provider and vehicle details.

Registration or a public profile alone does not qualify a transporter to accept live work.

02 / VERIFY

Complete the required checks.

Provide acceptable vehicle-use evidence and pass the insurance, safety and permit checks relevant to the work.

03 / ASSESS

Review the whole trip.

Consider collection travel, loaded distance, return trip, vehicle costs, load, handling and duration.

04 / OFFER

Choose a price and complete delivery.

Review the proposed commission and transporter share before offering. Payout follows the agreed verified completion process.

Proposed commission example

15% platform · 85% transporter

For an agreed fare of R1,000, the proposed split is R150 commission and R850 transporter share before processing fees, taxes, refunds, disputes and other agreed adjustments. The exact fee allocation and payout timing must be agreed before checkout is enabled.
