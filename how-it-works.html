A clear route from request to delivery

How it works.

Silent Frame Logistics is an independent introducer: it helps customers and businesses find independent transport providers in Krugersdorp and the West Rand. It does not own or operate vehicles or itself carry goods. This website is a preview; it does not make live introductions.

For customers

Request an introduction.

01 / ENQUIRE

Describe the load and route.

Share collection and delivery areas, load details and preferred timing. The current preview does not send a live request.

02 / INTRODUCE

Choose whether to connect.

If the introduction service is later enabled, the team will explain what information is shared before contacting a potential transporter. A match is not guaranteed.

03 / AGREE DIRECTLY

Set the service terms in writing.

The customer and independent transporter agree the scope, price, timing, payment, cancellation and responsibilities directly before proceeding.

04 / PROCEED WITH THE PROVIDER

The transporter carries the goods.

The transporter provides the agreed transport service. Silent Frame Logistics does not currently book, track, insure or pay for transport.

For transporters

Choose whether to take an introduction.

01 / REGISTER LATER

Provider applications are not open.

This preview does not accept transporter registration or collect vehicle documents.

02 / VERIFY BEFORE LAUNCH

Checks need a private process.

Any future checks must cover authorised vehicle use and the insurance, safety and permit requirements relevant to a job.

03 / SET YOUR OFFER

Assess your full operating costs.

Consider collection travel, loaded and return distance, vehicle costs, load, handling and duration. The transporter sets its own offer.

04 / AGREE A REFERRAL FEE

Know the terms before a referral.

Any introduction fee must be disclosed and agreed in writing before an introduction. No fee or payout is currently collected or made through the site.

Proposed introduction-fee example

15% introduction fee · 85% fare remainder

Illustration only: if an independent transporter agrees to a R1,000 fare and a separate written agreement sets a 15% introduction fee on that fare, the fee would be R150 and R850 would remain before costs and adjustments. This does not establish who owes the fee, when it is earned, how it is collected, or how refunds and disputes affect it. Agree those terms in writing before an introduction. No money moves through this site.
