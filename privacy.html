Privacy notice

Pilot draft: the marketplace is being prepared for a West Rand pilot. The operator must add its legal/business name and a monitored privacy contact before collecting real customer information.

Information collected

If the live enquiry service is configured, it receives your name, email address, phone number, request details, and approximate collection/delivery areas. Supplier sign-in uses your email address. Do not submit identity documents or payment details through this pilot.

How information is used

In configured preview mode, enquiry details are stored in the private marketplace database and forwarded to the pilot operator so the operator can respond. They are not displayed publicly. Supplier listing details are reviewed before appearing in public search.

If a future introduction feature is enabled, the operator must explain which request details would be shared with a potential independent transporter, obtain the required consent first, and share only the information needed for that introduction. That sharing feature is not active on this preview.

Storage and contact

The pilot operator must set and publish a retention period and access controls before launch. Until those are completed, do not use the site for sensitive or urgent requests. The privacy contact is not configured yet.

This is a launch draft, not legal advice. The business owner should review it against the actual data flows and South African privacy requirements before enabling real enquiries.
