Vehicle categories.

These categories describe the types of transport the marketplace plans to support. Final suitability must be checked against the specific vehicle, load dimensions, payload, route and applicable operating rules.

01

Bakkies

Small household items, furniture pieces, tools and local business deliveries.

Check payload, load restraint and bed dimensions.
02

Panel vans

Enclosed parcels, retail stock and goods that benefit from a covered load space.

Check internal height, volume and payload.
03

Small trucks

Bulky deliveries, building materials and smaller household moves.

Check body type, tail lift and loading access.
04

Medium trucks

Multi-item moves and larger commercial or supplier deliveries.

Check actual payload, route access and operating time.
05

Large & heavy-duty trucks

Higher-volume freight and heavier commercial loads.

Check licenses, route limits, permits and load securing.
06

Articulated & specialist vehicles

Freight that needs a specific trailer, configuration or handling capability.

Confirm permits, equipment and the full route plan.
Capacity figures are not guarantees. The accepted payload and usable dimensions must come from the registered vehicle and be checked for the particular load. Vehicle ownership or permission to use it does not by itself confirm safety, insurance or legal permission for every job.
Provider eligibility

Vehicle documents are the beginning.

The planned verification process checks ownership or authorised use, vehicle details, and the insurance, roadworthiness, driver credentials and permits relevant to the work. Sensitive evidence must be submitted through private, access-controlled storage. No job should be accepted until the backend confirms the provider and vehicle are eligible.
