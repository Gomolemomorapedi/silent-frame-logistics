West Rand pilot terms

Draft terms: the business operator must add its correct legal/business name and contact details, then review these terms before public use.

Our intended role

Silent Frame Logistics is an independent introducer intended to help customers and businesses connect with independent transport providers and, separately, product suppliers. It does not own or operate transport vehicles or itself carry goods. The live introduction service is not currently enabled. The draft wording must be reviewed against the actual business and final contracts before launch.

A website enquiry is not an introduction, quote, order, booking, transport contract, or promise that a provider, goods, price, or delivery time is available.

Separate agreements and responsibilities

If a customer and transporter choose to proceed, they should agree their transport service, exact price, collection and delivery details, timing, payment, cancellation and responsibility for loss, damage or delay in a written agreement directly with each other, unless a separate written arrangement says otherwise. The transporter is the proposed provider of the transport service. Silent Frame Logistics should not be described as carrying goods, guaranteeing a transporter, or arranging insurance unless the business's actual legal arrangements say so.

Any introduction fee, including the proposed 15% example, must have a clear written agreement identifying who pays, the calculation basis, when it is earned, collection method and treatment of cancellations, refunds and disputes. The percentages shown on the site are illustrative and do not by themselves create an agreement.

Listings and review

Listings submitted through the pilot are reviewed before publication. A review is not a guarantee of a supplier's identity, product quality, stock, licence, insurance, vehicle condition, or service performance. Report inaccurate or unsafe listings to the operator.

Payments and disputes

This preview does not collect transport payments, provide escrow, or pay transporters. Do not send payment-card or bank credentials through the website. The introduction service does not currently provide a transport booking, tracking, insurance or dispute-resolution process. Keep written records of any separate arrangement and seek independent advice if needed.

Contact

The operator must publish a monitored contact address before accepting pilot requests.

These terms remain a draft, not a legal shield or legal advice. Before collecting real enquiries or introducing parties, the owner should have a South African lawyer review the actual business structure, fee agreement, customer/provider contracts, consumer and privacy obligations, advertising, and dispute process.
