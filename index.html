Vehicles for different loads

Choose for the job, not just the distance.

Vehicle fit depends on the actual payload, dimensions, body type, route restrictions and the goods being carried.

Bakkies & panel vans

Small deliveries, furniture pieces, tools and business stock. Confirm usable load area and payload.

Small & medium trucks

Household moves, building supplies and larger business deliveries. Match dimensions and loading needs.

Large & heavy-duty trucks

Commercial freight and heavier loads, subject to route, licensing and vehicle restrictions.

Articulated & specialist

Oversized or specialised freight requires suitable permits, equipment and an agreed route plan.

Capacity is vehicle-specific. Category labels do not guarantee that a vehicle can safely or legally carry a particular load.
A real verification process

Proof of vehicle use is one part of trust.

Ownership documents alone do not establish that a provider is insured, safe, or authorised for every type of transport work.

01Confirm identity & account

Review account details and business information through an authorised, privacy-conscious process.

02Prove authorised vehicle use

Accept appropriate ownership, lease, rental or written owner-authorization evidence.

03Check job-specific readiness

Check insurance, roadworthiness and permits where relevant to the vehicle, route, load and work.

04Record decision & renewal

Keep reviewer, date, status and expiry information. Recheck before a provider can accept eligible work.

Recommended price for transporters

Price the whole journey.

A recommendation should be a configurable guide grounded in route and cost assumptions, never a guaranteed fare.

Route legs

Loaded road distance, travel to collection and agreed return travel, using a route estimate rather than straight-line distance.

Vehicle & time

Vehicle cost per kilometre, driver or operating hours, minimum call-out and number of booked days.

Load & handling

Weight, dimensions, additional stops, loading, unloading, waiting time and special handling.

Operating costs

Configured fuel, tolls and other relevant costs with clear assumptions and a sensible margin.

Pricing policy: the internal recommendation belongs on the eligible transporter view. The customer should see the transporter’s actual offer. Calibrate the rules from real route and completed-job data; do not show an invented instant quote.

How a transport request should work

From request to a clear offer.

Customer login and payment settlement are separate systems. The site must keep each status accurate.

01 / REQUEST

Share load, route and dates.

Include vehicle needs, dimensions, duration, stops and handling requirements.

02 / OFFER

Transporters assess the job.

Eligible providers review the route and their advisory cost estimate, then set an offer.

03 / CONFIRM

Customer accepts the offer.

Record the agreed price and terms before secure card checkout is enabled.

Pilot limits: live transport jobs, account roles, vehicle-document review, route pricing, checkout, split settlement, tracking and payouts are not yet configured.
